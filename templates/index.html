What is AQI?

The Air Quality Index (AQI) is a scale used to communicate how polluted the air is and what health effects might be a concern. India's AQI scale runs from 0 to 500 — the higher the number, the greater the health concern.

AirIQ uses community-submitted readings combined with a trained LSTM neural network to give you both real-time and predicted air quality for your area.
